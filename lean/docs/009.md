## Scope

Shared definitions (`GC/Combinatorics/GridCompilers/Basic.lean`): legal parent arrays `j_i < i`
(as the dependent function type `(i : Fin N) → Fin (i + 1)`), the Sequential NOR trace
`x_0 = 1`, `x_i = NOR(x_{i-1}, x_{j_i})`, the set of traces of length `N`, the active/reset row
map and the grid normal form `X_{i,j} = (X_{i,j-1} ⊕ α_i) ∧ (X_{i-1,j} ⊕ β_{i,j})` (words indexed
by `ℕ`). Row partitions and the cost `Γ` are in `RowPartitions.lean`, with `Γ` defined as the
least cost over all lists of positive block heights summing to `H`.

**Sequential NOR has exactly Fibonacci-many traces (009d).** The census theorem (Theorem 2.1) is
formalized in full with the paper's definitions: a word `(x_0, …, x_N)` is a trace iff `x_0 = 1`,
`x_1 = 0` and there are no two adjacent ones (`census_mem_iff`), and the set of traces of length
`N`, over all `N!` legal parent arrays, has exactly `F_{N+1}` elements (`census_card`, with
Mathlib's `Nat.fib`; the statement also holds for `N = 0`). The fibre theorem (Theorem 2.2) is
formalized in full: the fibre of a trace is the product `∏ A_i(x)` (`fibre_eq_piFinset`), the
factor sizes are as stated (`card_A`), every factor is nonempty, and `∑_x ∏_i c_i(x) = N!`
(`sum_prod_card_A`). Causal projection (Lemma 3.1) and the three counting inequalities of
Corollary 3.2 (`|𝓜| ≤ 2^{|D ∩ D_Tr|}`, `|𝓜| ≤ ∏_{i∈D} k_i`, `|𝓜| ≤ F_{N+1}`) are formalized.
Of the list-decoding results only the deterministic case of Theorem 4.2 (`δ = 0`, `B = L`) is
formalized, as an upper bound and an attaining construction, together with the identity
`∑_{n=1}^M F_{n+1} = F_{M+3} - 2`. Randomized decoders (Lemma 4.1, Theorems 4.2 and 5.4 for
`δ > 0`), the adaptive-query capacity `K_{N,q}` (Lemma 5.1, Theorem 5.2, Corollary 5.3), the
spectrum sandwich (Theorem 6.1, Corollary 6.2), Proposition 4.3, the bound `N ≥ h / log₂ φ` and
the potential-function remark are not formalized.

**Homogeneous Sequential NOR grids lie in NC¹ (009a).** The NC¹ compiler (Theorem 4.6), the
threshold-reset composition theorem (Theorem 5.2), Corollary 5.3, the embedding into Sequential
NOR (Proposition 3.4), the affine-product normal form and the degree bounds of Section 6 are not
formalized; no circuit, depth or uniformity statement is formalized. Formalized are the exact grid
facts the compilers rest on: active rows output words without adjacent ones (Lemma 3.1), a reset
row computes `L_i ∧ ⋀_{t ≤ j}(N_t ⊕ β_{i,t})` and outputs a threshold word (Lemma 3.2), the
orientation example (Remark 3.3), and for homogeneous templates: zero columns are threshold resets
with the stated closed form (Lemma 4.1), diagonal collapse `X_{i,j} = X_{i-1,j-1}` in dense slabs,
its iteration and the consequence that every slab cell copies a cell of rows 1–2 or columns
`c, c+1` (Lemma 4.2), no vertical `000` (Lemma 4.3, also for `i = 2`), and the closure of the
transfer class `𝒢_H` under composition with `|𝒢_H| ≤ 80` for `H ≥ 3` (Lemma 4.5 and the count in
Lemma 4.4). The classification of transfers (Lemma 4.4(a)–(d)) is not formalized.

**Three NC compilers for Sequential NOR grids (009b).** The three compilers (Theorems 3.4, 4.7,
5.3) and the anchor-image computation are not formalized. Formalized are: the bounds
`1 ≤ Γ ≤ min(H, W + 1)` (Lemma 3.1); Example 3.5(b) in its corrected form `Γ = min(H, W + 1)` for
alternating rows; the row facts that `(0^W, 0)` is the identity on Fibonacci words and
`(0^{W-1}1, 0)` an involution (the two injective rows of Proposition 4.5; the "only these two"
direction is not formalized), and that the pair `(1010⋯, 0)`, `(1^W, 1)` maps every Fibonacci
word to `0101⋯` (Example 5.4); the INDEX gadget (its last value is `y_t`) and Proposition 6.1(a)
and (c): a one-pass evaluator has at least `2^m` states after the payload, a context-independent
associative summary (semigroup or monoid) has at least `2^m` elements, and for `d × d` matrices
over a finite semiring `K` with `|K| ≥ 2`, `2^m ≤ |K|^{d²}` and `d ≥ √(m / log₂|K|)` (the bound
as corrected in the paper). Proposition 6.1(b) (randomized, Fano), Lemmas 4.1–4.2 (greedy
normalization, `T³ = T`), Proposition 4.3, Theorem 4.4, Proposition 4.6, the common-core
compiler, Lemma 5.1, Proposition 6.2 and Proposition 6.3 are not formalized.

**A runwise portfolio of NC grid compilers (009c).** The portfolio theorem (Theorem 3.2, an
`FNC^k` statement) is not formalized. Formalized are: the finite-portfolio lemma (Lemma 3.1) in
abstract form (compilers are maps from blocks to an optional trace function; the first-passing
portfolio passes iff some compiler passes and is sound if all are); the lower bound
`Γ ≥ min(g, d + 1)` (Lemma 4.1); `Γ ≤ 2` for the second run of Proposition 4.2 (`L` rows `I`
then `L` rows `Q`); and `Γ ≤ min(H, 1 + s)` for common cores (Proposition 5.1). The row facts of
Proposition 2.4 are the 009b theorems above. The full strictness statement (Proposition 4.2), in
particular membership in the collar class and the large-`M` parameter estimates, is not
formalized.

**Prefix-OR defeats treewidth and curvature measures (009e).** Formalized are: the masking
bijection with its explicit inverse and the invariance of mixed derivatives (Lemma 6.1); for
masked prefix OR, `⋁_{i≤e} y_i = ⋁_{i≤e} x_i`, `y_e = x_e ⊕ ⋁_{i<e} x_i` and the completeness of
the curvature hypergraph (Theorem 6.2, without the `NC¹` claim); the correctness of the
zero-mask reduction behind Theorem 6.3 (not P-completeness itself); for the reverse-prefix
family, `z_r = z_1 ∨ z_2` for `r ≥ 3` and an explicit `K_{k,k}` minor model in the moral graph,
`k = ⌊(h-1)/2⌋` (Theorem 4.1) — treewidth is not in Mathlib, so `tw(K_{k,k}) = k`, minor
monotonicity, the `Ω(√n)` bound and the record count are not formalized; for the triangle grid,
the record count `3r² + 4r`, every vertex cover of the moral graph has at least `2r²` vertices,
and the edges `A_iB_j` form a `K_{r,r}` (Theorem 4.2; the treewidth consequence is not
formalized). Replay (Proposition 2.1), relation fusion (Proposition 3.1), Propositions 5.1–5.2 and
all complexity statements are not formalized.
