# Tabulation, wavefront and frontier methods

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Parallel Simulation Where Only Nested Reads Cost Depth](../../preprints/Parallel-Simulation-Where-Only-Nested-Reads-Cost-Depth-November-3-2026/paper.pdf)
- [NOR Shift Registers Are Tiling Rotations](../../preprints/NOR-Shift-Registers-Are-Tiling-Rotations-November-3-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Constant-Frontier Computations Are in NC](../../preprints/Constant-Frontier-Computations-Are-in-NC-November-3-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Strict P-Completeness Reduces to the Control Trace](../../preprints/Strict-P-Completeness-Reduces-to-the-Control-Trace-November-3-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Four Barriers to Speeding Up Circuit Evaluation](../../preprints/Four-Barriers-to-Speeding-Up-Circuit-Evaluation-November-3-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

All five papers use one machine model, the normalized append-only machine. It is defined once in Lean, with `none` playing the role of the symbol ⊥. The real execution is defined for all stages, and statements restrict to `t ≤ N` where the horizon matters. Work, depth and PRAM costs are not formalized in any paper. In particular, no NC, SP or logspace statement is made in Lean.

**Parallel simulation (010a).** The global table operator `ℋ` is formalized, together with its prefix-composition form (`ℋ(Z)_t = (F_t ∘ ⋯ ∘ F_1)(C_0)`) and the read-adaptivity rank. The main theorem (Theorem 4.2) is proved in full: for every candidate transcript and every `r`, every stage of rank `≤ r` is exact after `r + 1` applications. Lean drops the hypothesis `Z_0 = C_0`, which is not needed. Also proved are the unique fixed point (Lemma 4.3, with a horizon) and the correctness of the stopping rule (Corollary 4.4). For the copy program, Proposition 5.1 is proved in full: `a(t) = ⌊(t−1)/d⌋`, the iterate from the all-zero candidate is exact on `[0, N]` iff `N ≤ r d`, the first exact round is `A_N + 1`, and `C*_t = (t, H_in[(t−1) mod d])`. The costs of Lemma 3.2 and Corollary 4.4, the doubling remark and the conditional consequence for `P ⊆ SP` are not formalized.

**NOR shift registers (010b).** The tiling-rotation theorem is formalized in full. This covers Lemma 2.2 (uniform-lag sequences are wavefronts), Lemma 3.2 (unique cyclic extension, `π` injective on tilings), Lemma 3.3 (two-sweep landing in the cyclic `10`/`100` tilings), Theorem 3.4 (`F_D(π w) = π(ρ w)` and `F_D^{D+1}(F_D^2 x) = F_D^2 x`) and Corollary 3.5 (`x_i = w_{(i+3) mod (D+1)}` for `i ≥ 2D`). Periodicity `x_{i+D+1} = x_i` is proved from `i ≥ 2D − 1` on, one index earlier than the paper states. Also proved are the complement-conjugation Lemma 4.1, the collapse Lemma 4.2 for `P`, `Q`, `A` and `F³ = F²` for all six collapsing rules, and Lemma 5.1 (non-affine iff `NOR(u⊕a, v⊕b)⊕c`, with a unique triple). The polarity-gauge conjugacy Theorem 5.3 is proved for uniform-lag grids in reading order: the gauged slab is a homogeneous `H`-wavefront. So is the optimality part of Proposition 5.6 (the greedy gauge cover is minimal). The prefix-scan Lemma 2.3, the linear-work/log-depth compilers (Corollaries 3.6, 5.4, Theorems 4.3, 5.5), the affine case of Theorem 4.3, the computation of `e_H(a)` and the P-completeness remark are not formalized.

**Constant-frontier computations (010c).** The internal live frontier, the compact-configuration graph, its edges and layer reachability are formalized. Proved are Lemma 3.2 (reachable nodes are exact), Lemma 3.3 (layer `t` is reachable iff `f(a, a+t) ≤ f`), the resulting criterion of Theorem 3.5 that the reachable layers are exactly those with `a + t ≤ e_f(a)`, and the exactness part of Corollary 3.6. Also proved are monotonicity (Lemma 2.3), restartability (Lemma 4.1), packing (Lemma 4.2) and minimality of the greedy epoch cover (Proposition 4.4). On the moving-frame side, Lean proves the conjugation Lemma 5.3 (with the support statement), one link of the event chain of Theorem 5.4 (the state stays put until the first later event whose source is the current state, then takes its target), and the cycle-plus-transposition formula behind Corollary 5.9 (`c^{-r} τ c^r = (−r, 1−r)` and `x_t = y_t + r_t`). The graph-size bound (Lemma 3.4), Boolean matrix squaring, all NC/depth bounds (Theorems 3.5, 4.3, 5.6 and Corollaries 3.6, 5.8, 5.9 as complexity statements), the successor-graph construction (Lemma 5.5) and pointer jumping are not formalized. The full multi-link event chain is obtained by iterating the formalized link, but it is not stated as a single theorem.

**Control trace (010d).** Normalized register–stack machines are formalized as append-only machines. Registers are indexed by an arbitrary finite type with designated registers `x_0, x_1, x_2, x_3`, and the row-0-zero condition is not needed. Proposition 2.4 (control-trace sufficiency) is proved: `q_t` is the prefix composition of `δ(·, b_t)`, and acceptance depends only on the control trace. Theorem 3.1 (triangularization) is proved: given the address–control trace, the register values satisfy `y = L y + d`, with `L` and `d` built from the trace and the known numbers, and `L` strictly lower triangular in time. Lemma 3.2 (nilpotent doubling) is proved in any ring. Theorem 3.3 (payload reconstruction) is proved as the identity `y = S_R d`, valid for every `R` with `N ≤ 2^R`. Fact 4.1 (Reinhardt), Theorem 4.2 (the conditional transfer), Proposition 5.3 (MCP/USIARV), the `O(1)`-nonzeros-per-row count, the PRAM costs and the integer bit bound are not formalized.

**Four barriers (010e).** Lean proves the extensional-summary barrier (Proposition 5.1). For the feature-dimension barrier it proves Lemma 4.2 (`c` and `τ` generate `S_m`), Theorem 4.4 (an invariant subspace containing a nonconstant function has dimension `≥ m − 1`) and Theorem 4.3 in both the linear (`r ≥ m − 1`) and affine (`r ≥ m − 2`) forms, over every field and every `m ≥ 2`. For the fixed-lag copy barrier, synchronous correction with radius `L` (Definition 2.1) is formalized for general machines. Theorem 2.2(a) is proved: the candidate value at relative stage `t` is `1` iff `t ≤ (r+1)d`, so the block is exact after `r` rounds iff `m ≤ (r+1)d`, which is `χ_L = ⌈m/d⌉ − 1`. Theorem 2.2(b) is proved as the lower bound `Σ_j ⌈m_j/d⌉ ≥ ⌈N/d⌉` over every partition, attained by blocks of length `d`; the minimum over `(B, σ)` is not written as a Lean definition. For the identity-padding barrier, affine–product circuits over `𝔽₂`, the multiplicative Strahler rank and exact Newton iteration are formalized. Proved are Lemma 3.1 (`z·z = z`), the ladder's values and ranks, and Theorem 3.2 in full (exact Newton delay: `z_k` is exact exactly from step `ρ(z_k) + 1 = k + 1` on). Proposition 2.3 (residue formula for the copy program) is the 010a statement `copy_real`. The padding construction (Theorem 3.3, Corollary 3.4), the block-matrix compiler (Proposition 4.1), Corollary 4.5, and the four-Russians Theorem 5.2 and Corollary 5.3 are not formalized.
