## Scope

All results are stated over the field `ZMod 2`. The SPAI model (stages with a probe address, a controller update and an affine data update, and the run of a list of stages) and affine decision summaries (leaves with an affine guard, an exit state and an exit map; exactness) are defined in `GC/Computability/SCLadder/Basic.lean`. No complexity class (SC, NC, logspace reductions, uniformity, circuit depth) is formalized anywhere in this family.

**008a (An SCᵏ-complete problem with an NC³ fragment).** Formalized: the correctness of the configuration encoding behind the hardness half of Theorem `thm:complete` (a machine that reads, writes and moves on a bit tape is simulated step by step by a time-invariant SPAI instance; its matrices are diagonal and differ from the identity in at most one diagonal entry); exactness of the transcript leaves (Lemma `lem:leaf`, shared with 008d); the feedback-free case of Proposition `prop:feedbackfree` (the block acts as `x ↦ (q, Mx + c)` with `q, M, c` independent of the data); pair exactness (Lemma `lem:pairs`); exact syndrome recompression (Theorem `thm:recompress`): if every pair-guard row lies in the row space of a full-row-rank `B_u`, then the `2^{ρ_u}` syndrome leaves are exact; and the invariant-observation-space reduction of Proposition `prop:invariant`. Not formalized: the Turing-machine normal form, padding and logspace computability of the reduction, the membership half, Corollary `cor:equiv`, the canonical hierarchy `LR` with its NC³ bound (Theorem `thm:lr`), all depth and uniformity statements, and the examples.

**008b (Knowledge compilation gives NC fragments of SC).** Formalized: leaf pullback and leaf conjunction of affine atoms (Lemmas `lem:leafpull`, `lem:leafand`), including singular maps, with the rank bound `rank(BM) ≤ rank B`; conjunction of decomposable nodes and preservation of determinism (Lemma `lem:vtops`); the transfer-pullback identity with pairwise disjoint terms (Lemma `lem:transfer`); the equality barrier (Proposition `prop:pfeq`: an accepting piece row inside the diagonal forces full column rank, and covering the diagonal of `𝔽₂ⁿ × 𝔽₂ⁿ` needs `2ⁿ` rectangles); the residual-state core of the ordered-equality barrier (Proposition `prop:ordeq`); the one-rectangle bound `|A||B| ≤ 2^{n-1}` for inner product (Lemma `lem:rect`); the count `(2ⁿ-1)2^{n-1}` of pairs with `a·b = 1`; and the inner-product barrier (Proposition `prop:vtip`: at least `2ⁿ-1` rectangles). Not formalized: the generic hierarchy theorem and the six class theorems (PF, Path, VT, AVP, SepTR, RF-TW) with their depth bounds, the canonical cap-checked algorithms, the selected-row-parity and other gain families, the separator construction and the `K_{n,n}` barrier, and the treewidth claims.

**008c (Inner product has affine-cover number 2ᵐ).** Formalized in full: Theorem `thm:ip`. Every affine subspace (Mathlib `AffineSubspace`) contained in `H_m = {(a,b,a·b)}` has at most `2^m` points; the `2^m` affine subspaces `{(a,b,a·b) : b}` lie in `H_m`, cover it and are pairwise disjoint; and the affine-cover number (least number of affine subspaces inside `H_m` covering it) equals `2^m`. Also formalized: exact projection through a left nullspace (Lemma `lem:proj`) and the state-injectivity core of the INDEX bound (Proposition `prop:index`, for a node whose state determines acceptance together with the outside slots). Not formalized: the AU and ALVA languages and their NC³ theorems, the bag recursion of tree decompositions, Proposition `prop:tri`, Proposition `prop:bip`, and the vtree-automaton model itself.

**008d (Logarithmic feedback in SC² lies in NC²).** Formalized: the unique-transcript lemma (Lemma `lem:unique`): for every entering state and data vector exactly one transcript has a successful forced run whose affine guard holds, and for it the block ends at `(q_{u,τ}, M_{u,τ}x + c_{u,τ})`; successful transcripts have one bit per feedback-sensitive stage and guards with one row per such stage, so there are at most `2^h` of them; and the transcript leaves form an exact affine decision summary. The forced run is defined stage by stage, which is equivalent to the segment recursion of the paper. Not formalized: Theorem `thm:block` and Corollary `cor:sparse` (NC² circuits, uniformity), Proposition `prop:mass` beyond the `2^h` count, and the examples.

**008e (Few nonlinear gates per path put circuits in NC).** Formalized: the triangular product identity (Lemma `lem:product`): for strictly lower triangular `M` and `2^s ≥ n`, `∏_{k<s}(I + M^{2^k})` is the two-sided inverse of `I + M`; the algebraic core of Theorem `thm:regions` (the local equations `x = Mx + Nz + c` of a region hold if and only if `x = (SN)z + Sc`); and Lemma `lem:boundary` for the non-affine depth (monotone along references, strictly increasing at barriers). Not formalized: Theorems `thm:extract` and `thm:ladder`, Corollary `cor:ladder` (depth bounds, FNC classes, uniformity), the macro grammar, the algebraic-normal-form test, the region graph, and the register example.
